## Evidence: what blocks the domain switch

Judged by the worst moment, not the average. A visitor who clicks one level deep hits the gap immediately.

1. **The library is 2 books, and both are the same project.** Sanity holds exactly 2 project docs (`star-island`, `star-island-2`); the build's SSG output and the live `/case-studies` grid confirm it. The hero shelf sells a 7-book library (Raghsa, Cazouls, Dillido, Manhattan, NY Penthouse, Windsor, 803 Hunter). Clicking through breaks the promise. **This is the single largest gap.**
2. **Hero book covers are placeholders.** `public/images/01-07.jpg` are stand-in renders per `public/images/README.txt`. They also leak into the Services deck, which reuses 03/07/05.jpg instead of dedicated imagery.
3. **Team photos are grey boxes** on `/about`. The page's own copy claims "the people you meet are the people doing the work" next to empty rectangles.
4. **`/journal` 404s by design.** Both `journal/page.tsx` and `journal/[slug]/page.tsx` call `notFound()` unconditionally. Meanwhile the homepage Journal slider shows 5 mock entries (`lib/journal-mock.ts`) whose "Read" affordance leads nowhere. Either ship 2+ real entries and open the route, or remove the slider's link affordance for launch.

## Non-blocking, ship as-is

- Services deck copy/images hardcoded (not CMS). Fine for launch; move to Sanity later if edit frequency demands it.
- Peer band: 1 CMS quote + hardcoded verified quotes and 24-firm client roster. Real content, acceptable hybrid.
- Legal pages are minimal but present and render.
- Concept/About/Contact copy is CMS-backed with sane fallbacks.

## Domain-switch checklist (what must exist before oaki.studio points here)

1. 7 final hero covers in `public/images/01-07.jpg` (real project renders, editorial grade).
2. Minimum 4 of 7 case studies live in Sanity with real FASES media and copy (7 is the target; 4 makes the library credible, and remove or reorder hero books that lack a case study behind them).
3. 3 dedicated Services deck images (concept sketch energy, proof detail, campaign hero).
4. Team photos on about, or an art-directed alternative (the grey boxes cannot ship).
5. Journal decision: open with 2 real entries or disable the slider link affordance.
6. Housekeeping: remove `?freezehero`, delete `HomeHero.tsx`, fix or accept lint errors, push/prune branches, commit handoff docs.
7. Vercel: create project, set env vars (`NEXT_PUBLIC_SANITY_PROJECT_ID`, dataset, `SANITY_API_READ_TOKEN`, write token if Studio deploys, `RESEND_API_KEY`), verify Studio route auth, then point DNS.
8. Redirects from old-site URLs (`/portfolio/` is indexed) to new routes; new metadata/OG images.
