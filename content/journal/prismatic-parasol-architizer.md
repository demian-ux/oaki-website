At the 2026 Architizer A+Awards, Prismatic Parasol received a Special Mention in the Unbuilt Sustainable Non-Residential category. It follows an Editor's Choice win at the 2025 Architizer Vision Awards and a Platinum at the 2025 Design Skills Awards. The pavilion gathers sun and rain for a village that needs both.

oaki produced the competition renderings; the published credit reads "Demián Szklar and his team at Oaki Studio." Congratulations to Michael Kokora, Marcus Carter, and Miranda Lee of Object Territories.
