At the 2026 AIA Florida Design Awards, Oak House received a Citation Award for Unbuilt Work. The house bends its roofline and its rooms around the trees; nothing on the site was asked to move. The award listing runs on a single image: the courtyard, the oak at its center, the house holding back around it.

oaki produced the renderings for Oak House between 2024 and 2026, including the image credited on the award page. Congratulations to Wesley Kean and the team at KODA, whose work made the image worth making.
