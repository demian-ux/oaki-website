Bally's Chicago topped off in May 2026: a $1.7 billion casino and hotel, the city's first, with an opening set for spring 2027. When its interiors reached the press in December 2025, they arrived as renderings. Among the venues is the food hall, designed by ICRAVE.

oaki produced the food hall's renderings with ICRAVE through 2026. Congratulations to Andrew Delgado, Lisa Johnson, and the ICRAVE team.
