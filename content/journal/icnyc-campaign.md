ICNYC is raising $5 million to renovate its building: a prayer hall, a co-working area with a cafe, a wellness center. The campaign shows its future through images, floor by floor, from the dome to the minbar to the street facade.

oaki has produced the project's renderings since 2025, from the prayer hall interiors to the facade studies now in progress. Congratulations to Asifa Tirmizi and the ICNYC community.
