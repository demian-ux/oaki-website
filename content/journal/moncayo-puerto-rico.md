Moncayo is the project the real estate press is calling the Palm Beach of Puerto Rico: 400 residences from $3 million, an Auberge resort, a first phase arriving in 2027. Inside the masterplan, KODA designed two houses.

oaki produced the renderings for both in 2024. Congratulations to Wesley Kean and the team at KODA.
