On February 24, 2026, the Centre de Gestion de l'Hérault named Naos architecture winner of the competition for its new headquarters: a bioclimatic building organized around a central patio, with photovoltaics and Mediterranean planting. Building permit in May 2026, first stone in early 2027, delivery in 2028. The client's announcement runs on three images.

oaki produced the competition image set for Naos, including the three renderings on that page. Congratulations to the Naos team.
