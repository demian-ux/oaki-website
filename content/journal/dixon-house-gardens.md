The Dixon House sold for $13.5 million in December 2024. Its new owners kept the house, work of the architect who shaped Art Deco Miami Beach, and commissioned LandFluent to rethink the grounds around it.

oaki produced the garden renderings in 2026. Congratulations to Peter Anselmo and the LandFluent team.
