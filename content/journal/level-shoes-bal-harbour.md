WWD broke the news: Level Shoes, the Chalhoub Group's Dubai retailer, is coming to the US, and it chose Bal Harbour Shops for its flagship. The store is designed by KODA and set to open in 2027.

oaki produced the store's renderings with KODA over the winter of 2025 and 2026. Congratulations to Wesley Kean and the team at KODA.
