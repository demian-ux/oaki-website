At the 2023 AIA Miami Design Awards, MIAC, the Miami Art Center, won a Merit Award of Excellence in the Unbuilt category. The design keeps the market's bones and wraps them in a gabion facade built from fragments of demolished buildings.

In an unbuilt category, the images are how the jury meets the building. oaki produced MIAC's renderings in 2023. Congratulations to Wesley Kean and the team at KODA.
