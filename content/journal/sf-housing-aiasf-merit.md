AIA San Francisco's Housing+ competition asked architects to answer the city's housing crisis. Object Territories entered three schemes; Carved Terrace Block took a Merit Award, and the results were covered by The Architect's Newspaper and Architectural Review.

oaki produced the renderings for all three schemes in 2023. Congratulations to Marcus Carter, Miranda Lee, and Michael Kokora of Object Territories.
