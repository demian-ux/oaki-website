## Intro
On a headland above the Black Sea, The Point was envisioned by Gregory Tuck Architecture as a refined coastal retreat. Classical proportions, layered interiors and gardens that open toward the water shape a sequence of rooms conceived for both gathering and solitude, where each space keeps its own measure of light.

Oaki composed the imagery as a single day at the property. It begins in the open air of the garden pool and the bar, moves into the quieter rooms of the residences, and ends at the arrival court after dark. Guests, staff and the small traces of a day in use give the architecture its life, so that every frame reads as a moment within the same unhurried experience.

## Afterward
Years after delivery, Gregory Tuck recommended oaki, unprompted, to a developer planning a new resort. An architect staking his own name on the studio, years later, is the outcome.
