## Intro
AFT drew a market under the pines. A hall with a folded roof and a glass front, two long bars of shops and offices behind it, and the forest kept whole between them, the trunks running straight up through the balconies.

Six views walk the visitor in. The crossing from the road, the hall head on, the gallery under the planted balconies, the timber screen on the corner, the front from the parking, and the hall again at dusk with the lights on inside.

## Outcome
Every tree in the frames stands where the survey put it. The buildings were modeled once and the whole site rendered from the same model, so the six views agree with each other and with the plan.
