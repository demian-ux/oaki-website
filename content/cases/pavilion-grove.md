## Intro
Koda drew a house that hides under its own trees. A court where the lawn should be. A river where the path should be. A bedroom that opens its whole wall to the game below.

They asked for six images, quickly. Then they asked for the walk.

## Interlude
A still can hold a house. It cannot hold the sound of water under your feet, or the pause before a serve, or the fire catching as the light goes. The film had to be the thing the images could only promise.

We built the house once and kept it. Every frame of the film comes out of the same model as the stills. Same light, same afternoon, same leaves. Nothing borrowed, nothing stock. Four people live in it for eighty seconds. A game, a swim, a fire, a bed.

The walk went to Koda first as still frames with music, so the story was agreed before anything moved. Their note: let the gym and the bedroom breathe. We did.

## Outcome
The film arrived a day early. Koda showed it to their client the next morning. One note came back, a color on the court. The final cut went out on the Monday.

Eight weeks, first email to final frame. Six stills, one storyboard, one film.
