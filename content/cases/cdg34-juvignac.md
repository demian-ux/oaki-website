## Setup
The Centre de Gestion de l'Hérault, the public body managing local government staff for the department, held a competition for its new headquarters in Juvignac: a bioclimatic office building organized around a central patio, with photovoltaics and Mediterranean planting. Naos architecture entered.

oaki produced the competition image set for Naos.

## Tension
A public competition jury reads dozens of entries in a sitting. The images do not get a second look; they either make the bioclimatic argument at first glance or the entry drops out. Every frame had to work as both architecture and evidence: the patio as organizing idea, the planting as climate strategy, not decoration.

[GAP for Demi: the brief as it arrived, the model state, rounds, timeline.]

## Approach
Three frames, three scales. The patio view establishes the organizing idea, the wide frame places the building in its landscape with the photovoltaic roof legible, and the facade view holds the climate detail. Southern French light throughout, the hour that makes Mediterranean planting read as shade rather than garnish.

## Outcome
On February 24, 2026, the Centre de Gestion de l'Hérault named Naos winner of the competition. The client's public announcement runs on three images; all three are oaki's. Building permit in May 2026, first stone in early 2027, delivery in 2028. Source: the CDG 34 announcement.
