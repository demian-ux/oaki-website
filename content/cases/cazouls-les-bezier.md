## Setup
A public project in Cazouls-lès-Béziers, in the south of France: low pavilions organized around garden courts, by Naos.

oaki produced the competition image set in 2025.

[GAP for Demi: the program and the competition context.]

## Tension
Low buildings lose competitions to tall drawings. Architecture that keeps its head down, pavilions, courts, planting, has to win on atmosphere and ground-level truth, because it will never win on skyline. The images had to make modesty read as intent.

[GAP for Demi: the brief as it arrived, rounds, timeline.]

## Approach
The garden court leads, and the eye stays low: the sequence walks between the pavilions at the height a visitor would, ending on the built edge where material meets planting. Languedoc light.
