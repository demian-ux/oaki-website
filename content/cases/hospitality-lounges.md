## Intro
The designers behind these rooms make the places where travel slows down: lounges, bars, dining rooms and family spaces where every surface, light and seat is chosen to change how a few hours feel. The rooms gathered here belong to several projects and several years. What they share is the way they were made.

Oaki works alongside the design team through the whole design process, not only at its end. From the earliest plans, the studio builds each room as a working model and renders it as the design moves: testing ceiling geometries, finishes, lighting scenes and furniture against one another, so that options are judged as images rather than as samples. When a scheme settles, the same model carries it into the final frames. The pictures in this collection are therefore both the record of each design and the tool with which it was decided.
