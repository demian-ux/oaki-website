## Setup
Bally's Chicago is a $1.7 billion casino and hotel in River West, the city's first, topped off in May 2026 with an opening set for spring 2027. Among its venues is the food hall, designed by ICRAVE. When the interiors reached the press in December 2025, they arrived as renderings.

oaki produced the food hall's renderings with ICRAVE through 2026.

## Tension
Inside a story that size, a single venue's images have to do two jobs at once: hold their own as the food hall, and sit correctly inside the casino's larger press narrative. A frame that oversells becomes a target; a frame that undersells disappears next to the headline number.

[GAP for Demi: the brief as it arrived, rounds, timeline.]

## Approach
The hall leads at full width, then the sequence tightens: stalls, seating, counter, and finally the hall in service. Food-hall light, bright and specific, distinct from the casino floor around it.

## Outcome
The project's interiors reached the public as renderings, in coverage of a $1.7 billion opening set for spring 2027. Sources: casino.org on the topping off, Chicago YIMBY on the interior renderings.
