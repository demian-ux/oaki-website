## Setup
In 2023, 94 S Hibiscus existed as drawings. A new waterfront residence on Hibiscus Island by DN'A Design and Architecture, developed with JAE Development, and a plan to sell it ahead of completion with its own dedicated marketing site.

oaki was brought in for the landscape renderings.

## Tension
A buyer can read a floor plan. Nobody can read a garden. Planting takes years to grow into a photograph, and a waterfront lot with no green reads as concrete. The part of the house hardest to imagine was the part the marketing needed most.

[GAP for Demi: the brief as it arrived, what the drawings gave and what they left open.]

## Approach
The landscape frames were built to show the house already held inside its planting: the entry sequence, the pool terrace, the water edge, each at the hour that serves it.

[GAP for Demi: planting references, light decisions, rounds and timeline.]

## Outcome
The house was completed in 2024 and sold through its own marketing site. It closed at $18.5 million on May 29, 2026.

The landscape renderings are oaki's part of that story, made while the house was still drawings.
