## Setup
Some commissions come with a condition attached: the work can be shown, the life inside it cannot. Alderbrook is a family house by Studio ST Architects, with interiors by Maggie Rosenblatt, for clients who asked to stay unnamed.

This page honors that. No name, no address, no faces.

## Tension
A private house is the hardest kind of work to publish. Everything that makes it real, the family, the site, the particulars, is exactly what cannot be shown. Most studios solve this by not publishing at all. The work disappears with the secret.

[GAP for Demi: the brief as it arrived and the specific pressure of the commission.]

## Approach
The frames were chosen the way the commission asked: the house in its light, the rooms as spaces rather than possessions, nothing that places the site or names the people in it. What remains is the architecture and the way it holds a family's life without exposing it.
