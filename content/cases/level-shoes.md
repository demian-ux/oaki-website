## Setup
Level Shoes, the Chalhoub Group's Dubai retailer, is opening its first United States flagship: close to 2,000 square meters at Bal Harbour Shops, designed by KODA, set to open in 2027. WWD broke the news before ground was touched.

oaki produced the store's renderings with KODA over the winter of 2025 and 2026.

## Tension
When a global brand announces its US debut, the renderings are the debut. There is no store to photograph, and the brand's own standard, set across Dubai, is the bar the images are measured against. A frame that reads as "architectural visualization" instead of "Level Shoes" fails, even if the geometry is perfect.

[GAP for Demi: the brief as it arrived, rounds, timeline.]

## Approach
The sequence walks the store the way a client would: entry, main floor, the men's and women's areas, then the rooms that make this flagship particular, the whitespace gallery, the cobbler, the bar, the pool room. Retail light, not gallery light: the products and materials carry the same weight as the architecture.

## Outcome
The flagship was announced in WWD with the project set to open in 2027. Source: the WWD story on the Bal Harbour flagship.
