## Intro
A fast-moving competition pitch for one of the biggest names in finance, produced with iCrave, part of Journey, the practice behind work at JFK Terminal 5 and Sphere Las Vegas. Journey has a Technical Director of Visualization on staff. He brought oaki in.

Competition pitches do not move deadlines. The design develops while the images are being made, and the last views are always the most important ones. There is no room for a studio that needs the brief spoon-fed.

## Interlude
Constant communication, views uploading as they finished, the priority frames turned around inside the day.

The team's running commentary in the thread: "I see the views coming in, thank you so much" and, hours later, "okay this is amazing."

## Outcome
The pitch shipped on time. Within weeks Journey was back with the next project, and then another: "Got another one that needs renderings." The relationship is the outcome: the studio with visualization in-house keeps choosing oaki, project after project.
