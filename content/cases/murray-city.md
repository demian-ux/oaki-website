## Intro
A city asked for proposals on a block between its main avenue and a park. Maddproject had nine days to answer. A parking structure, a bar of shops and offices around a plaza, and a quarter of townhomes behind it, with the model still a massing and the townhomes still a sketch.

The set reads at three distances. The aerial puts the whole block into a photograph of the city, the mountains taking the top of the frame, so the council sees the plan in its place. The street view holds the plaza under the sign, by day and again by night with the lights on. The site plan draws the townhomes and their courts clean, for the diagrams to sit on top.

## Outcome
Nine days, brief to final, with two rounds on every image. Two sections and a set of townhome studies went with it, so the presentation could explain the houses without drawing them.
