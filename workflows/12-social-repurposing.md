# Social Repurposing Workflow (12)

**Status:** draft (crop lane proven on NY Penthouse; carousel/reel templates pending first post)
**Last updated:** 2026-07-02
**Owner:** Demian (publishing is a business act); production by anyone with this doc

## Purpose & when to use

Nothing is produced twice: every social asset derives from case-study material already made (covers from 07, stills from F2, mockups from 08, clips from 10). Run after a case study ships, to produce its feed package. HARD GATE: held case studies (DONE-HELD, pre-site-launch) do not get teasers; publication order follows the business rule that prestige is parked until the site is live.

## The package per case study

| Piece | Source | Format |
|---|---|---|
| Cover reveal | 07 cover or the 08 book mockup | 4:5 feed (1080x1350) |
| Carousel (the book, paged) | 4-6 frames in the case study's arc order | 4:5 per slide |
| Reel / story crops | hero frames, later the 10 motion clips | 9:16 (1080x1920) |
| Reel cover card | the cover crop | 9:16 |
| Copy | tease + phase lines from the 05 copy doc, re-cut | oaki-copywriting rules |

## Procedure

1. Crops via sharp from the MASTERS (never from web derivatives): 1080x1350 and 1080x1920, `position: attention`, eyeball every crop.
2. Sequence the carousel in the case-study arc order (hero first); the feed tells the same story the page does, shorter.
3. Captions: start from the 05 tease and phase bodies; cut for feed rhythm; credit line included exactly as granted; location wording per the clearance block.
4. Name per F3 (`{project}_soc_{subject}-{45|916}_v##`), store in the project `social/` folder.
5. Demi approves the package and schedules; nothing auto-posts.

## Quality bar

Same grade family as the site (no separate "Instagram look"); credit + exclusions per clearance on every piece; captions pass oaki-copywriting; a crop that beheads its subject is redone, not shipped.

## Output spec

`social/` folder per project: crops named per F3, one caption file. Publishing schedule lives with Demi.

## Reference example

NY Penthouse (2026-07-02): 4 crops produced (terrace + living, 4:5 + 9:16 each) in `NY Penthouse/social/`. Captions pending Demi's copy pick.

## Known failure modes / gotchas

- Teasing held material (banned; see the gate above).
- Cropping from the 200KB web files instead of masters (visible on retina feeds).
- Instagram's own compression punishes >30% saturation pushes; the F2 discipline is also a platform-survival rule.
- The old bio/positioning is still live on the accounts (audit item); the first case-study post should land after or with the bio fix, not under "sell faster" branding.
